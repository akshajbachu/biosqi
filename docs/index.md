# biosqi

**Explainable signal-quality assessment for biomedical recordings.**

biosqi divides ECG, PPG, or EEG recordings into windows, computes transparent
signal-quality indicators (SQIs), and returns:

- a calibrated 0–100 usability score for each window;
- a boolean mask suitable for downstream processing;
- evidence for likely artifacts such as clipping, flatline, baseline wander,
  mains interference, and poor periodic consistency;
- machine-readable JSON/CSV and a local interactive dashboard.

## Scope and safety

biosqi measures properties of a recording. It does **not** diagnose a patient,
determine whether a physiological pattern is normal, or replace review by a
qualified clinician. The bundled model is validated on the datasets stated in
[Validation](validation.md); performance may not transfer to a different
sensor, population, acquisition protocol, or artifact distribution.

## Design principles

1. **Evidence before labels.** Every quality decision exposes the indicator
   values that contributed to it.
2. **Calibration before confidence.** A score is presented as a probability
   only when its calibration procedure and validation population are stated.
3. **No hidden preprocessing.** Sampling rate, profile, window length,
   overlap, threshold, and model version are included in every report.
4. **Reproducible claims.** Published metrics are generated by
   `benchmarks/run_all.py` from public datasets.
